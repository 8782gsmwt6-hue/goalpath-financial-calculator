Research & assumption library 📚

Use authoritative sources to pressure-test the figures you enter.

Inflation

Inflation reduces future purchasing power. For long-range planning, test more than one assumption rather than relying only on the latest 12-month figure.

BLS Consumer Price Index ↗ BLS Inflation Calculator ↗

Investment returns

Historical results vary dramatically by asset mix and time period. Run conservative, base, and optimistic scenarios. Do not treat a historical average as a promised annual return.

Investor.gov Compound Interest Calculator ↗ NYU Historical Returns Dataset ↗

Home-price appreciation

National averages may not reflect your target city, neighborhood, property type, insurance costs, taxes, or maintenance needs.

FHFA House Price Index ↗ FHFA Regional Datasets ↗

Mortgage rates

Mortgage rates affect affordability but are separate from the investment return used to grow your down-payment fund.

Freddie Mac Current Mortgage Rates ↗ Freddie Mac Historical Rates ↗

Investment fees

Expense ratios, advisory fees, trading costs, and account fees reduce your net return. Enter the combined annual percentage you expect to pay.

Investor.gov: Understanding Fees ↗

Increasing contributions

An annual contribution increase can model raises or a deliberate savings step-up. Use a rate you could realistically maintain.

Investor.gov Savings Goal Calculator ↗

Down payments and buying costs

A second-home goal may require the down payment, closing costs, reserves, furnishings, maintenance, taxes, insurance, and possibly higher lending requirements.

CFPB Home-Buying Resources ↗

Important limitation

This app performs deterministic projections. Actual returns arrive unevenly, investments can lose value, taxes may apply, and goal costs can change. Use the results as planning ranges—not financial advice or guarantees.
